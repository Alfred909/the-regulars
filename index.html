The Regulars

Everyone on Gai Fong Street has somewhere to be: noodles at the stall, a browse at the pawn shop, home before ten. Three of the regulars are here for you. Find them before they find you, then slip into Yaki's godowns. Stay out of sight, lift the stair key from the breakroom, and take Yaki down on the roof. Keep your hands clean of everyone else.

Watch for the glint. A hitman's glasses catch the light when he's watching your back.
